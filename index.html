How scoring works
  • Before the merge: pick one castaway from each tribe you think goes home. Each correct pick is worth 100.
  • After the merge: rank your three most likely boots. 150 if your #1 goes, 75 for #2, 40 for #3.
  • Finale: rank three castaways to win the season, same 150 / 75 / 40.
  • Episode title bonus: every week, guess who says the line that becomes the episode title for +5.
  • Picks lock automatically at the deadline shown. No pick, no points.
